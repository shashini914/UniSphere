# Working Agreement

The **Working Agreement** outlines the UniSphere team’s expectations and guidelines for effective collaboration.  
It was created **by the team, for the team**, and can be updated as needed throughout the project.

---

## Communication & Availability

- **Primary communication channel**: Microsoft Teams.  
- Team members are expected to **check messages at least once daily** and respond within 24 hours.  
- Each member will share their **general availability** to coordinate work effectively.  
- If a member will be unavailable for more than 2 days, they must notify the team in advance.

## Meetings

- **Weekly team meetings** every **Monday at 5:00 PM** on MS Teams.  
- Additional ad-hoc meetings can be scheduled as needed.  
- If a member **cannot attend**, they should notify the group beforehand and review meeting notes afterward.

## Development Practices

- All work must be done in **feature branches**.  
- **Pull requests** must be submitted for code review before merging.  
- No **direct commits** to `main`.  
- Follow agreed-upon **coding standards and scripting practices**.  
- Team members must **test their code locally** before submitting PRs.  
- Avoid “breaking” other members’ code; if issues arise, communicate immediately.

## Repository & Dataset Updates

- Keep the **main branch stable and deployable** at all times.  
- When updating datasets or shared files, clearly document changes in the issue or PR.  
- Communicate major data structure changes during meetings or via Teams.

## Missed Deadlines / Ghosting

- Consistent last-minute submissions or long periods of unresponsiveness (“ghosting”) negatively affect the team.  
- If someone is struggling, they should **communicate early** so support can be provided.  
- Repeated issues will be escalated to the professor if needed.

## Agreement Updates

- This Working Agreement is a **living document**.  
- The team may revise it at sprint reviews or as new situations arise.
